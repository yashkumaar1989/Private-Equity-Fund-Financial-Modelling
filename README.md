
# Private Equity Closed-End Fund | Integrated Financial Model

### Fund Financial Modelling • GP/LP Waterfall • Carried Interest • Investor Returns

An integrated financial model designed to evaluate the economics, investment performance and investor distributions of a **closed-end private equity fund** managed by an Asset Management Company (AMC).

The model brings together fund-level financial projections, investment deployment, debt financing, management fees, operating expenses, GP/LP distribution waterfalls and individual investor returns.

It demonstrates the financial modelling framework used to assess fund performance, investment profitability and the allocation of economic returns between Limited Partners (LPs) and General Partners (GPs).

---

## 1. Project Overview

Closed-end private equity funds typically operate with a fixed investment commitment, a defined investment period and a predetermined fund life, potentially subject to extensions.

Investors commit capital to the fund, which is drawn down over the investment period and deployed into portfolio investments. Investment proceeds, dividends and exit distributions are subsequently allocated between investors and the fund manager according to the agreed partnership economics.

This model integrates the full fund lifecycle, from initial capital commitments and investment deployment through portfolio realisations, investor distributions and final fund liquidation.

### Key Modelling Capabilities

- Fund size, investment period, maturity and extension assumptions
- Capital commitments, drawdowns and investment deployment
- Portfolio-level investment cash flows and exit valuations

## 3. GP/LP Partnership Distribution Waterfall

A central component of the model is the partnership distribution waterfall, which determines how investment proceeds are allocated between Limited Partners and General Partners.

The framework incorporates:

### Return of Capital

Allocation of distributions towards the recovery of investor capital contributions.

### Preferred Return / Hurdle

Calculation of investor preferred returns based on the applicable hurdle-rate assumptions.

### GP Catch-Up

Modelling of GP catch-up arrangements, including alternative treatments of catch-up before or after principal recovery.

### Carried Interest

Calculation of performance-based compensation payable to the GP under the modelled partnership economics.

### Residual Profit Distribution

Allocation of remaining distributable profits between LPs and GPs according to the applicable sharing arrangements.

The waterfall enables an assessment of how different fund performance outcomes affect investor and fund manager returns.

## 5. Fund Performance Metrics

The model includes financial performance and return calculations covering:

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| Gross IRR | Internal rate of return before applicable fund-level costs and investor allocations |
| Net IRR | Investor return after applicable fees, expenses and carried interest |
| MOIC | Multiple on Invested Capital |
| TVPI | Total Value to Paid-In Capital |
| DPI | Distributions to Paid-In Capital |
| NAV | Net Asset Value of unrealised portfolio investments |
| Capital Drawdowns | Capital contributions throughout the investment period |
| Investor Distributions | Cash proceeds distributed to investors |
| Carried Interest | Performance-based GP compensation |

Together, these metrics provide a view of fund profitability, realised returns, unrealised value and investor performance.
